# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Startup operations leads and chiefs of staff coordinating an in-person offsite
for employees traveling from different geographies. They work under time and
budget pressure, reconcile incomplete inputs, and remain accountable for every
commitment. Their primary task is to move from a fragmented brief to one
feasible, reviewable, and safely authorized operating plan.

## Product Purpose

Offsite Captain coordinates travel constraints, agenda dependencies,
preparation work, lodging, meeting rooms, and team activities into a single
validated offsite plan. Success means the operator can understand what the
system found, resolve exceptions, verify the exact cost and commitments, and
authorize simulated reservations without surrendering control to the agent.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The product communicates like a capable operations
partner: concise about routine work, explicit about risk, and never theatrical
about AI involvement.

## Anti-references

- Chat-first experiences that hide the plan, constraints, or current state in a
  transcript.
- Generic SaaS dashboards made from repeated cards without a workflow hierarchy.
- Travel marketplaces that prioritize browsing, promotions, or urgency tactics.
- Unexplained AI output, false certainty, or language implying that reservations
  exist before human authorization.
- Decorative motion, novelty controls, and dense technical detail that distract
  from the operator's decision.

## Design Principles

1. Make the operating plan the primary object, not the conversation.
2. Show why a plan is feasible before asking for trust.
3. Separate proposal, authorization, and reservation as unmistakable states.
4. Put exceptions where the operator can resolve them in context.
5. Preserve human control with exact scope, cost, expiry, and recovery language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every workflow must support keyboard navigation, visible
focus, semantic headings and landmarks, reduced motion, sufficient contrast,
and status communication that does not rely on color alone. Dates and times
must include the relevant timezone. Dietary and accessibility needs are
operational requirements, not secondary profile metadata.
